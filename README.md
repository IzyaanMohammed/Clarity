Clarity: CBSE Student OS
Transforming passive studying into active, daily mastery.
Live deployment: claritystudy.vercel.app

The Vision
Clarity is a proactive, all-in-one study ecosystem custom-built for CBSE students in Grades 8 to 12 navigating the NCERT curriculum. Instead of just storing notes or offering generic quizzes, Clarity operates as a comprehensive Student Operating System, gamifying the daily routine, identifying exact knowledge gaps, and preparing students for board-level rigor.

Core Features & Architecture
The Daily Mission Engine
Removes the confusion of daily planning by curating tailored study goals based on upcoming exams, weakest topics, and high-weightage NCERT chapters.

Consistency Streaks: Built-in tracking to turn study consistency into a habit loop.

Dynamic Task Prioritization: Balances physics derivations, mathematics practice, and humanities revisions seamlessly.

Adaptive Practice and Exam Readiness
Clarity tests conceptual depth rather than simple rote memory.

NCERT Blueprint Mapping: Questions strictly mirror official CBSE patterns, including Competency-Based Questions, Case-Based Studies, and Assertion-Reasoning types.

Readiness Index: A real-time data visualizer that aggregates mock test scores, time-per-question metrics, and accuracy data to predict exam readiness.

OCR-Driven Smart Workflows
Converts physical clutter into digital intelligence.

Handwritten Notes and PYQs: Scan a photo of a textbook problem, school pre-board exam paper, or a scribbled formula sheet.

Instant Digitization: Clarity converts the image into clean text and automatically extracts the core topics for targeted review.

AI-Driven CBSE Tutor
An AI companion optimized specifically for the NCERT syllabus.

Step-by-Step Breakdown: Guides students through complex derivations and mechanisms line by line instead of just providing the final answer.

Board Evaluator Mode: Grades practice answers against official CBSE marking schemes to highlight where a student might lose partial marks.

Tech Stack
Clarity is engineered for speed, responsiveness, and minimal latency.

Frontend: Next.js (React) deployed on Vercel

Styling: Tailwind CSS / Shadcn UI for a distraction-free interface

Database and Authentication: Supabase / Prisma (PostgreSQL)

AI Integration: LLM APIs with custom system prompts mapped directly to the NCERT curriculum

Roadmap

Phase 2: Peer-to-peer accountability study rooms and collaborative board prep
